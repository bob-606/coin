## 1. Why coins from EU are a perfect fit

Collectibles is one of 6 hero categories on the event page.

Market in 2026:

* Coins = ~half of all eBay Collectibles revenue. ~75k sold / 30 days, ~$11.6M, avg ~$154.
* Searches for collectible coins +30% June 2025 -> June 2026, historic coin listings +40%.
* Aug 18, 2026: eBay expanded **Authenticity Guarantee to coins $500+** (graded US + graded World + raw US, authenticated by PCGS, free).
* May 2026: new **mandatory condition rules** for Coins: US 253, World 256, Canada 3377, Ancient 4733, Medieval 18466. Must choose `Graded [company + grade]` or `Ungraded [Uncirculated, About Circulated, Extra Fine, Fine, Below Fine]`.
* eBay Live for coins is growing - e.g. $11,400 graded 1883 Trade Dollar sold live.

Baltic angle: EU euro collector coins, Baltic / Soviet / German / Scandinavian history, ancient/medieval. Inventory US buyers can't source locally.

eBay reach: 136M active buyers across 190+ markets.

## 3. EU -> World coin cheat sheet for eBay

### Listing

* No counterfeits. Replicas/medals similar to euro coins must be clearly marked and comply with EU Reg 2182/2004.
* Ancient/medieval: check national cultural heritage export license - Latvia/Lithuania/Estonia all control archaeological objects. Don't list without provenance papers.
* Use standardized condition, cert numbers, both sides + edge photos, weight/diameter.

### Fees

* ~13.6% final value + $0.40 per order + 1.65% international surcharge. Price that in.

### Taxes - shipping FROM EU

* EU seller shipping from EU to EU private buyer <€10k/year total: domestic VAT rules. Over €10k: destination VAT via OSS.
* Shipping EU -> US/UK/AU: export, 0% VAT in EU, buyer pays import VAT/duty on arrival. eBay collects automatically in many cases (IOSS up to €150 into EU, UK, AU GST etc).
* For second-hand margin scheme for collectibles, talk to `1stopVAT` - they are a Connect Sponsor and will be there.

### Shipping - sponsors to talk to

* `Orange Connex (Global Partner), Omniva, DPD, Lietuvos pastas, Latvijas Pasts, Asendia, SpeedPAK`
* Ask: tracked + insured + signature for coins <$500 vs >$500, loss rate to US, DDP options.
* Coins are small/high value: never untracked letter. Use bubble mailer + cardboard flip, HS code 7118.90 / 9705 for collections, full customs declaration.
* US inbound Authenticity Guarantee flow is US-only for now, but ask eBay if EU sellers can opt-in for $500+.
